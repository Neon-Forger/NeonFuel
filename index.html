HOW TO INSTALL

NeonFuel isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks one extra question the first time.

  1. Download the APK
    Tap the download button on your Android phone.
  2. Open it
    Open NeonFuel.apk from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install
    If Android says "unknown apps", tap Settings and turn on "Allow from this source" for your browser.
  4. Make your profile
    Pick a username (not your real name!) and add your friends by username.
